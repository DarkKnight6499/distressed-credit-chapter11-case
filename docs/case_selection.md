# Case selection

Selected: Party City Holdco Inc.'s 2023 Chapter 11 case. The first suggested candidate provided an accessible EDGAR chain: petition/RSA report, petition capital structure, filed plan, recovery supplement, updated valuation and projections, confirmation, and emergence. These primary HTML documents could be opened and read directly.

Use the July 21 third amended plan as the analytical vintage. Its supplement explicitly reports class recovery ranges and separates direct equity from subscription-right value. Its accompanying cleansing materials include the updated valuation that the supplement omits. This makes the difference between a priority waterfall and a negotiated restructuring measurable.

The September confirmation concerns a later fourth amended plan. The study does not relabel July recoveries as confirmed or realized recoveries. The later annual report is used only to cross-check historical financing terms, not to introduce later operating results into valuation.

Coverage is incomplete. The original disclosure statement intentionally omits its liquidation exhibit, and the Kroll docket returns HTTP 403. Docket-dependent liquidation work stopped. No primary liquidation values were recovered, and none were estimated. Other suggested cases were not fully audited; no comparative completeness ranking is claimed.

Links, retrieval dates, document sections and missing fields are recorded in [source verification](source_verification.md). The source chain supports the verified case components; it does not establish completeness of the claims register.
