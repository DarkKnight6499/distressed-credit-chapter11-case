# Party City restructuring: retrospective desk note

This is a retrospective study, not investment advice. The analytical vintage is the third amended plan dated 2023-07-21. Confirmation and emergence occurred later; projected recoveries below are not realized distributions. [F34](source_verification.md#f34).

**Situation.** Liquidity pressure led to a debt-for-equity restructuring. The analytical question is whether value accrues to the old claim, to new financing, or to subscription rights. A simple enterprise-value waterfall cannot price all three. Keep financing participation and old-claim consideration separate.

**Capital structure.** Petition funded principal totals USD 1,451.2 million: USD 424.3 million ABL, USD 911.7 million secured notes and USD 115.2 million unsecured bonds. The ABL has priority over receivables and inventory; the notes have first liens on other collateral. Both secured-note issues share the modeled fulcrum rank. July ABL floors replace the unverified petition split in the numerical model. [D04](source_verification.md#d04), [F02](source_verification.md#f02), [D05](source_verification.md#d05), [D06](source_verification.md#d06).

**Valuation and fulcrum.** The stated EV range is USD 450 million to USD 775 million. The arithmetic base is USD 612.5 million. Filed assumed exit net debt is USD 513 million, giving base reorganized equity of USD 99.5 million. The standalone priority model gives secured notes 4.18% at base and 22.00% at high EV. Those results depend on the DIP proxy and omitted estate costs. Secured notes are the plan fulcrum; at low EV, the model instead places the marginal loss in ABL. [F25](source_verification.md#f25), [D01](source_verification.md#d01), [F27](source_verification.md#f27), [D07](source_verification.md#d07), [D02](source_verification.md#d02), [D03](source_verification.md#d03).

**Filed consideration.** Projected secured-note recovery is 0.0%-7.60%. The filing separately attributes 0.0%-0.08% to direct equity and 0.0%-7.53% to subscription rights. Rounded components do not exactly add to the reported ceiling. General unsecured recovery is projected at 1.0%-2.5%, despite the strict-priority model paying the bond subset nothing. Rights, dilution and the negotiated GUC pool explain why priority alone does not reproduce the filed economics. [F21](source_verification.md#f21), [F22](source_verification.md#f22), [F23](source_verification.md#f23).

**Trade framing.** ABL exposure expresses a refinancing and collateral-coverage view, with borrowing-base and execution risk. Secured notes express a recovery-and-rights view: distinguish the floating and fixed coupons, and assess participation cash, dilution, transfer restrictions and exit security liquidity before comparing claims. Unsecured bonds express a settlement-pool view, with uncertain allowed claims, trust expenses and timing. Old common has no distribution under the filed treatment. No entry prices or return estimates are supplied because verified trading data is absent. [F24](source_verification.md#f24).

**What changes the view.** Revise for stronger cash generation, weaker inventory realizations, a larger priority reserve, a different DIP takeout, changed rights economics or a larger unsecured denominator. A complete collateral and claims reconciliation would matter more than a finer EV increment. Liquidation downside remains unverified because the source attachment is omitted and the claims-agent docket is blocked. The model cannot establish a liquidation floor.
